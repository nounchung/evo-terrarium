# R4 Validation

Status: implementation complete; automated and Jason-mode visual gate required before merge

## Scope

- Deterministic temperature, rainfall, soil moisture, day phase and four seven-day seasons.
- Seed-scheduled and player-placed drought, flood, disease and wildfire.
- Regional plant, hydration, energy, health and reproduction effects.
- Visible pressure radii, recovery events, and lasting meadow or grassland habitat change.

## Acceptance evidence

- Vitest proves climate/event determinism, legacy migration, bounded history, recovery and long-run food-web survival.
- Playwright covers climate-lab discovery and exact pause/restore semantics while placing a pressure.
- Production build and lint run from a clean checkout.
- Jason-mode visual review checks climate hierarchy, event visibility, placement affordance, contrast and world-first composition on the deployed preview.

## Safety boundaries

- Seed scheduling allows no more than two simultaneous events.
- Regional radii range from 110 to 150 world units; historical records are capped at sixteen.
- Disaster damage is local, time-bounded and uses the serialized seed stream.
- Previous snapshots receive safe climate, empty disaster and hazard defaults.
