## Implemented scope

- Terrain, organism, atmosphere and social rendering are split out of `WorldCanvas.tsx`; the simulation Worker and authoritative organism model are unchanged.
- Six transparent raster sprites cover grazer/hunter juvenile, adult and older stages.
- The six source images are packed without resampling into one 3×2 GPU atlas, preventing WebKit multi-texture upload races.
- Age derives the life stage. Genes continue to control size, hue and body proportion.
- Forage, hunt, flee, rest, mate, drink and migrate map authoritative `behaviour` values into bounded postures and cues.
- Reduced motion keeps state cues while removing decorative bob and trails.
- Distant zoom and populations above 115 organisms select a reduced-detail path.
- `?r9qa=species` provides a deterministic renderer-only seven-creature scene without mutating Worker state.
- Canvas instrumentation exposes renderer, count, detail, life stages, behaviour cues, QA layout/scale, reduced-motion state and creature-build duration.

The remote browser and GitHub software renderers throttle animation to approximately 1 FPS, so their absolute FPS is not treated as physical-device evidence. R9.1's same-run relative terrain gate remains active, and R9.2 adds an independent 100 ms creature-build ceiling.

Quality run #48 retained the unchanged R9.1 relative gate: Living reached 95.7% of Classic median FPS on desktop (4.4 vs 4.6) and 102.8% on mobile (11.0 vs 10.7) in the throttled runner. Living terrain builds were 24.4 ms desktop and 40.0 ms mobile, both below 100 ms.
